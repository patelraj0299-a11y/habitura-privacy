About your data

Habitura · Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: everything you create in Habitura stays on your phone. There are no accounts and no Habitura servers, so we cannot see, read or sell your habits, tasks or notes. You decide if and where a backup is made.

Where your data is stored

Your habits, tasks, checklists, notes, timer sessions, reminders and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never sent to us.

  • Habitura has no sign-up and no user accounts.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for Habitura, so your data is not copied to your Google account automatically.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear its data, everything stored on the phone is removed.

What Google services receive

To keep the app working and free, a few Google services receive limited technical information. None of it includes the content of your habits, tasks or notes.

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: anonymous usage events, crash reports and general device details (model, Android version, app version, country).
  • Firebase Remote Config: an app installation ID, used to turn features or offers on and off.
  • Google AdMob (free version only): advertising ID and device information to show ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Google Play Billing: handles the Premium purchase. We never see your payment details.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Because your data lives only on your phone, make a backup before you change or reset your device.

  • Open Settings → Backups (or Account and Backups in the menu) and tap Create backup.
  • Android asks where to save the file. You can keep it on the phone or choose a cloud folder you use, such as Google Drive.
  • On the new phone, install Habitura, open the same screen and choose Import backup to restore from that file.
  • We never receive your backup file. Keep it somewhere safe, because anyone who has the file can read your data.

CSV export

You can export your data as a spreadsheet (CSV) from the backups screen. The file is saved only where you choose.

App lock

If you set a PIN, it is stored on your phone as a secure hash, not as plain text. Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android, and Habitura never receives your fingerprint.

Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play, linked to the Google account you bought it with. Install Habitura on any device signed in to that account and Premium is restored automatically. Your habits and tasks are not part of the purchase and are not moved by it; use a backup file for that.

How to delete your data

  • A single habit: open the habit, go to the Edit tab and tap Delete habit. This removes the habit and all its history.
  • Everything on the phone: Android Settings → Apps → Habitura → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Backup files: delete them from the folder or cloud drive where you saved them. We do not have a copy.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or turn off personalised ads.
  • Analytics or crash data held by Google for us: email us and we will help you request deletion.
