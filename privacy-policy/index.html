Privacy Policy & Terms

Habitura · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Habitura has no user accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone. A few Google services collect limited technical data so we can fix crashes, understand how the app is used, show ads in the free version and process purchases. We never sell your data.

1. Information the app collects

To keep Habitura stable and to improve it, the app sends some anonymous technical information to Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics:

  • How the app is used: when it is opened, which screens are viewed, which buttons are tapped and how long a session lasts.
  • Crash and error reports.
  • General device details: model, manufacturer, Android version, app version and country.

We use this to see which features people use, find and fix bugs, check that the app works on different devices and decide what to build next. It does not include your name, email address or anything you write in your habits, tasks or notes, and we do not use it to identify you.

The app also uses Firebase Remote Config to switch features or offers on and off without an app update. This uses an app installation ID and basic device information only.

2. Ads

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings.
  • Buying Premium removes all ads.

3. In-app purchases

  • Habitura Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. It is not a subscription.
  • Google handles the payment. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Premium is tied to the Google account you bought it with. Install the app on any device signed in to that account and Premium is restored automatically.
  • Premium is a personal licence. It cannot be transferred, resold or moved to another Google account.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

4. Your habits, tasks and backups

  • Your habits, tasks, notes, timer sessions and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see or access them.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for Habitura, so this data is not copied to your Google account.
  • You can create a backup file or export a CSV. The file is saved only where you choose, and we never receive it. Keep it somewhere safe, because anyone who has it can read your data.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything stored on your phone.
  • If you set an app lock PIN, it is stored on your phone as a secure hash. Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android, and the app never receives your fingerprint.

5. Google Play services

Habitura relies on Google Play services, which may collect information used to identify your device. Please read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps.

6. Children

Habitura is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Not professional advice

Habitura helps you plan and track habits, tasks and timed sessions. Example categories (such as fitness, health, sleep or food) and any suggestions in the app are general information meant to help you stay organised. They are not medical, psychological, financial or any other professional advice. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new exercise, diet or health routine. You use the app at your own discretion, and we cannot promise any particular result.

8. When you email us

If you contact us through Contact us in the app, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, and we delete support emails once they are no longer needed, at the latest after 24 months.

9. Sharing and selling

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share information only with the Google services named above, which process it to provide their service to us.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Under some laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), showing personalised ads can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can limit this by turning off personalised ads, as described in section 2.

10. How long data is kept

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics: according to Google Analytics retention settings (user-level data for up to 14 months).
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days in Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Support emails: up to 24 months.

11. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

12. Legal bases (EU / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process data on these legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the app features and Premium purchases you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports, basic usage analytics and keeping the app secure.
  • Consent: personalised ads. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy settings.

13. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the CCPA, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, receive a copy of it, object to or restrict how it is used, and withdraw consent.

  • Delete your data: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Habitura → Storage → Clear data.
  • Ads: change your consent in the app, or reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings.
  • Any other request: email us at the address below. Because Habitura has no accounts, we may ask for your app installation ID or other details to find data held by Google services for us.
  • You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

14. International transfers

Google services may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google protects these transfers with safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

15. Links to other services

The app and this policy link to services we do not control, such as Google Play, your email app and Google's privacy pages. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and it applies from that date. For important changes, we will also let you know in the app or on the Google Play listing. Please check this page from time to time.

17. Agreement

By using Habitura, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it.

18. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@habitura.co.in.
